**Travellers Titles** is a lightweight, immersive client-side Minecraft Fabric mod that brings an RPG-style adventure feel to your exploration. Every time you step into a new biome, a cinematic popup title greets you, accompanied by custom audio cues. 

---

## ✨ Features

* **Cinematic Biome Popups:** Displays smooth, RPG-like title cards on your screen whenever you cross into a new biome.
* **Immersive Sound Effects:** Plays a default or custom sound effect every time a new title triggers.
* **Custom Sound Support:** Easily pack your own custom audio files via resource packs to personalize your travels.
* **Detailed Toggles & Display Options:** Configure and toggle extra biome parameters on the fly, including:
  * Biome Temperature displays
  * Biome Rarity details
* **Fully Customizable UI:** Tweak title and subtitle size, color, confirmation delay, notification cooldown and screen position to fit your preference.
* **Client-Side Only:** Runs entirely on the client, meaning you can use it on any multiplayer server without requiring server-side installation!

---

## 🎵 Adding Custom Sounds

You can use your own custom sound files by creating a lightweight resource pack:
1. Create a resource pack with a memorable namespace.
2. Inside the `sounds` folder, place your audio files formatted as `.ogg`.
3. Configure them to trigger upon entering new regions for a completely unique atmospheric experience.

---

## ⚙️ Configuration

Travellers Titles comes with a robust configuration menu allowing you to:
* Change specific HUD elements based on preference (like temperature and rarity markers).
* Change the position of the title card from centre to top.
* Edit custom biome sounds or remove our 4 default configured biomes.

---

## 📥 Installation

1. Ensure you have the **Fabric Loader** installed for Minecraft **26.3**.
2. Download the latest release of **Travellers Titles** from the [Releases page](../../releases).
3. Drop the `.jar` file into your `mods` folder.
4. Launch the game and start exploring!
